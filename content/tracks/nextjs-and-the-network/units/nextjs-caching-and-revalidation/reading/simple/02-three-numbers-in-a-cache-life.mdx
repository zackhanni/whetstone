title: Three numbers in every cacheLife — stale, revalidate, expire
---

`cacheLife` sets a cached entry's lifetime with three timings, each aimed at a different reader:

- **`stale`** — the **browser**. How long Next's client router reuses the entry without asking the
  server during navigation.
- **`revalidate`** — the **server**. After this, the next request still gets the cached entry
  immediately, and the server regenerates it **in the background** (stale-while-revalidate).
- **`expire`** — the server's hard limit. After this long **with no requests**, the next request
  waits for fresh content.

The built-in profiles (Next 16 `cacheLife` reference):

| Profile | `stale` | `revalidate` | `expire` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `default` (no `cacheLife` call) | 5 min | 15 min | never |
| `seconds` | 30 s | 1 s | 1 min |
| `minutes` | 5 min | 1 min | 1 h |
| `hours` | 5 min | 1 h | 1 day |
| `days` | 5 min | 1 day | 1 week |
| `weeks` | 5 min | 1 week | 30 days |
| `max` | 5 min | 30 days | 1 year |

A timeline for `cacheLife("hours")` on a product list, cached at 09:00:

- **09:40**: served from the cache; nothing runs.
- **10:10**: past `revalidate`. This visitor gets the 09:00 list instantly; the server regenerates
  in the background, and the next visitor sees the new one.
- Nobody visits from 10:15 until the next day at **11:00**: past `expire` with no traffic in
  between, so this visitor **waits** while the list is rebuilt.

For a one-off, pass an object instead of a name. Missing fields inherit from `default`, and
`expire` must be longer than `revalidate`:

```ts
cacheLife({ revalidate: 300, expire: 3600 }); // stale comes from `default`: 5 min
```

<!--more-->

Very short lifetimes change where the work happens. A cache with `revalidate: 0` or an `expire`
under five minutes (of the presets, only `seconds`) is **short-lived**: Next leaves it out of
the prerendered page and treats it as a dynamic hole that runs at request time. The
`cacheLife` docs for Next 16.4 add a `stale` under 30 seconds to that list. If a short-lived cache is nested
inside another `use cache` with no explicit `cacheLife`, the prerender fails with an error, so
that a short lifetime buried in an imported component can't silently shorten the outer cache.
