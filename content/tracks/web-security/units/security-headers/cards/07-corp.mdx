---
id: corp
title: Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy — who may embed your files
---

```http
Cross-Origin-Resource-Policy: same-site
```

Set on a **resource** (an image, a script, a JSON file), not on the page that loads it. It tells the
browser to block other sites from loading it with a `no-cors` request: an `<img>`, a `<script>`,
or `fetch(url, { mode: "no-cors" })`.

| Value | Who may load it |
| --- | --- |
| `same-origin` | only pages on exactly your origin |
| `same-site` | your origin and sibling subdomains |
| `cross-origin` | anyone (what a public CDN or embeddable widget needs) |

It closes a gap CORS doesn't: CORS controls *reading* responses through `fetch`, but `no-cors`
loads such as `<img>` and `<script>` happen without it. Useful for private, per-user files, and
it's how a cross-origin resource opts in to being loaded by a page that sends
`Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp`.
