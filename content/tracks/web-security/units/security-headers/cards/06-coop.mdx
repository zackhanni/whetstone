---
id: coop
title: Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy — cut the link to whoever opened you
---

```http
Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin
```

When another site opens your page with `window.open()`, the two windows can normally keep
references to each other. COOP puts your document in its own browsing context group unless the
other side is same-origin, so a cross-origin opener can't reach your window, and the browser may
isolate it in a separate process. MDN: this "prevents a set of cross-origin attacks referred to as
XS-Leaks".

- `same-origin` is the strict value (OWASP's recommendation).
- `same-origin-allow-popups` keeps working popups you open yourself, such as OAuth or payment
  windows that have no COOP of their own.
- The default, with no header, is `unsafe-none`.

Paired with `Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp` it makes the page cross-origin isolated,
which unlocks `SharedArrayBuffer`.
