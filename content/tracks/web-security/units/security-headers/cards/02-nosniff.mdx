---
id: nosniff
title: X-Content-Type-Options — no MIME sniffing
---

```http
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
```

Tells the browser to believe your `Content-Type` instead of guessing from the bytes. Without it, a
user-uploaded file served as `text/plain` that happens to contain HTML could be rendered as a page
on your origin.

With `nosniff`:

- a response loaded as a **script** is blocked unless its type is a JavaScript MIME type, and one
  loaded as a **stylesheet** unless it's `text/css`;
- other responses, navigations included, use the declared `Content-Type` as-is.

It's the only valid value, and it has a precondition: every resource must be served with the right
`Content-Type`, or your own scripts and styles get blocked.
